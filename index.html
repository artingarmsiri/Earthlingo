About Earthlingo

Earthlingo is an interactive linguistic atlas designed to make exploring world languages, accents, and dialects visual, engaging, and accessible.

How to interact:

  • Rotate & Zoom: Drag to spin the Earth. Scroll to zoom in and examine borders.
  • Click any country: Flies the camera right to that country and slides open a comprehensive breakdown of its official, indigenous, and regional languages.
  • Explore Accents & Dialects: See phonetic quirks, famous slang, and regional variations (e.g. Cockney vs Scouse in the UK, Chilango vs Norteño in Mexico, Swiss German vs High German).
  • Listen to Phrases: Click the speaker button in the Phrasebook to hear authentic greetings spoken aloud using the browser's Text-to-Speech engine!
  • Origin & Migration Arcs: Click the curved arc icon at the bottom bar to animate prehistoric homelands (Urheimats) and historical migration waves with timeline scholarship.
  • Map Overlays: Click the stacked layers icon at the bottom bar to overlay Country Names, Primary Languages, Families, Writing Scripts, or Local Greetings directly on nations — labels adapt automatically as you zoom.
  • Filter by Language: Click language chips to highlight shared nations across continents.
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Press / to search, Space to pause/resume rotation, and Esc to close menus.
