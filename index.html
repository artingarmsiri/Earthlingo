About Earthlingo

Earthlingo is an interactive linguistic atlas designed to make exploring world languages, accents, and dialects visual, engaging, and accessible.

How to interact:

  • Rotate & Zoom: Drag to spin the Earth. Scroll or pinch to zoom in and examine borders.
  • Color Globe by Language, Family, or Script: Switch coloring mode directly from the bottom-left legend or Map Overlays popover — Auto-sync matches land colors whenever you view Families or Scripts overlays.
  • Minority & Spoken Language Spotlight: Click any spoken language card inside a country drawer (e.g. Kurdish, Quechua, Tamil, Yoruba, Catalan) to spotlight every country where it is spoken.
  • Guess the Greeting Quiz & Compare Mode: Test your ear and phonetic skills in Quiz by globe, continent, or writing script, or open Compare to contrast two countries side-by-side.
  • Origin & Migration Arcs: Click the curved arc icon to animate prehistoric homelands (Urheimats) and historical migration waves with timeline scholarship.
  • Map Overlays & Shareable Deep-Links: Overlay Countries, Languages, Families, Scripts, or Local Greetings directly on the globe, or use the Share icon in any drawer to copy a link to the exact view.
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Press / to search, Space to pause/resume rotation, and Esc to close active panels.
