About Earthlingo

Earthlingo is an interactive offline-ready 3D linguistic atlas exploring 242 nations’ living languages, scripts, accents, word histories, and historical spheres.

Interactive Features:

  • Color Globe by Language, Family, Script, or Diversity: Spot hyper-diverse hotspots (Papua New Guinea, Nigeria, India, Indonesia, Vanuatu) using the Greenberg LDI layer and toggle Endangered Pins.
  • Meet the Writing Script: Click any Script tag inside a country profile for an interactive breakdown of Abjads, Abugidas, Hangul Jamo bits, Japanese Furigana, Cyrillic & more.
  • Word Routes & Origin Arcs: Trace ancient Wanderwörter (Tea overland chá vs maritime tê, Coffee, Sugar, Orange, Zero, Mama) or macro language family dispersals.
  • Historical Time-Scrubber: Scrub back to 2000 BCE, 100 CE, 1000 CE, and Pre-1492 CE to see indigenous Americas, pre-colonial Australia, Old Norse Greenland & classical spheres.
  • Quiz, Daily 5 & Linguistic Passport: Play Global, Script Detective, Dialect Trivia, or today’s seeded 5-round Daily Challenge with shareable emoji grids, while logging explored stamps in your Passport.
  • Travel Trip Cheat-Sheet: Star countries (e.g. Spain → Morocco → Italy) to build, copy, or print a pocket greeting & accent guide — installable offline as a PWA.

Zero-build architecture — press / to search, Space to rotate, and Esc to close panels.
